// Origin Story

Why We Started Remolda

The AI consulting market is full of firms that will sell you a strategy deck and disappear. We saw too many mid-market companies — smart businesses with real problems and real budgets — get burned by consultants who were better at PowerPoint than at shipping software.

Remolda was founded to fill that gap. We're a team of engineers, data scientists, and operators who have built AI systems at scale — not just advised on them. We've seen what works and, more importantly, what doesn't.

Our name comes from "remold" — to reshape, to transform with intention. That's what we do for our clients: we don't add AI on top of broken processes. We help them think differently about how their business operates, then build the systems to make it real.
