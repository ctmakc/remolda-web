## [H2] Fraud Detection and Risk Mitigation for Brokers

As claims become more digital, the risk of sophisticated fraud increases. AI acts as your digital private investigator.
*   **Anomaly Detection:** AI identifies patterns across multiple claims that a human eye would miss—such as recurring names, addresses, or suspicious photo metadata—protecting the broker’s reputation with carriers.
*   **Predictive Risk Scoring:** Every incoming claim is assigned a "Confidence Score" based on historical data, allowing your senior adjusters to focus their time on high-complexity or high-risk files.
