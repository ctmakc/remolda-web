## [H2] Beyond the Resume: Autonomous Talent Qualification

A resume is a static, often inflated document. Our AI agents don't just read resumes; they conduct **Initial Intake Assessments**.

*   **Intelligent Sourcing:** Our agents scan public and private databases (LinkedIn, GitHub, specialized forums) to find "Passive Candidates" who aren't even looking but are a perfect fit.
*   **Contextual Screening:** AI conducts the first-round chat or text interview, asking deep technical or situational questions that reveal a candidate's true expertise.
*   **Bias Mitigation:** By focusing on objective data points and skills, our AI reduces the unconscious bias that often plagues human-led initial screenings.
