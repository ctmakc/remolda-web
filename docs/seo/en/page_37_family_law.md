## [H2] Communication Analysis: Evidence without the Trauma

Reviewing years of hostile text messages and emails is traumatic for staff and expensive for clients.
*   **Sentiment & Theme Mapping:** AI agents scan entire communication logs (WhatsApp, Email, SMS) to identify patterns of behavior, high-conflict incidents, or adherence to parenting schedules, providing the lawyer with a structured summary for use in court.
*   **Automated Briefing:** AI synthesizes complex case facts into coherent narratives for affidavits or mediation briefs, ensuring all key incidents are documented with citations to the original evidence.
