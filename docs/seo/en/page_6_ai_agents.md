## [H2] What are Autonomous AI Agents for Business?
**[AEO Block]** Autonomous AI Agents are specialized software entities powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) that are given the authority to perform multi-step actions within a business environment. Unlike simple automation scripts, agents can handle ambiguity, learn from context, and use tools—such as CRMs, email clients, and ERP systems—to complete complex goals like "qualify a lead and book a meeting" or "reconcile these 500 invoices against our bank statement." ## [H2] Top Use Cases for AI Agents in Canada

### 1. The Sales Development Agent (SDA)
The SDA monitors your lead sources 24/7. It researches the lead’s company, crafts a personalized first touch, handles the initial objections, and only puts the meeting on your calendar when the lead is "Sales Ready."
*   **Impact:** 400% increase in outbound volume without hiring a single BDR.

### 2. The Operational Triage Agent (OTA)
The OTA lives in your "Operations" inbox. It reads every incoming request, identifies the intent (Invoice? Support? RFQ?), pulls the relevant data from your legacy systems, and prepares the response for human approval or sends it autonomously.
*   **Impact:** 80% reduction in response time (SLA).

### 3. The Financial Reconciliation Agent (FRA)
The FRA monitors your bank feeds and your accounting software. It automatically matches payments to invoices, identifies discrepancies, and sends polite "overdue" reminders to clients.
*   **Impact:** Zero-touch accounts receivable.
