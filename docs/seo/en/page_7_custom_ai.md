## [H2] RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): The "Knowledge" Layer

The biggest problem with generic AI is "Hallucination"—the tendency to make things up when it doesn't know the answer. RAG solves this.

*   **The Vector Brain:** We turn your company’s unstructured data (PDFs, spreadsheets, technical manuals, emails) into a "Vector Database." 
*   **Grounded Truth:** When you ask our systems a question, they first search your private database for the most relevant facts and *then* use the LLM to synthesize the answer based **only** on those facts.
*   **Result:** High-precision, verifiable AI that provides citations for every answer. No more guessing.

## [H2] LLM Fine-Tuning: The "Expertise" Layer

Sometimes, RAG isn't enough. If you need the AI to actually *reason* like your best expert or *speak* exactly like your brand, we use fine-tuning.

1.  **Domain Mastery:** We train models on your specific technical datasets, ensuring they understand the "shorthand" and nuances of your industry.
2.  **Brand Consistency:** We fine-tune models on your best sales and marketing copy, ensuring every AI-generated output sounds exactly like it came from your top senior partner.
3.  **Process Mastery:** We can fine-tune small, efficient models to master one specific task (like code review or medical coding) at a fraction of the cost of larger models.
