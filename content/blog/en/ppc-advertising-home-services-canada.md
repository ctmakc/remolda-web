# PPC Advertising for Canadian Home Services: Plumbing, HVAC, and Electricians

When a pipe bursts at midnight or a furnace fails on a -30°C Winnipeg evening, homeowners don't scroll through social media or wait to receive a mailer. They pick up their phone, search "emergency plumber near me" on Google, and call one of the first results they see. For home services businesses in Canada — plumbers, HVAC contractors, electricians — paid search advertising is the most direct path to that moment of maximum buying intent.

But the landscape has changed significantly with the expansion of **Google Local Services Ads (LSA)** — a format that sits above traditional Google Ads and operates on a pay-per-lead rather than pay-per-click model. Understanding when to use LSA, when to use traditional Google Ads, and how to run both effectively is the foundation of a high-performance home services PPC strategy.

## Google Local Services Ads vs. Google Ads for Home Services

### What Are Google Local Services Ads (LSA)?

LSA are a distinct Google advertising product that appears **above all other search results** — above traditional Google Ads, above the Local Pack, and above organic results. They display as cards with:
- Your business name and phone number
- Your Google star rating and review count
- The **Google Guaranteed badge** (or Google Screened for certain professional services)
- Your service area

The critical difference from traditional Google Ads: **you pay per lead** (a qualifying call or message), not per click. If someone clicks your LSA card and doesn't call, you pay nothing. If someone calls but it's a wrong number or outside your service area, you can dispute the charge.

### LSA Verification and the Google Guaranteed Badge

To appear in LSA, your business must pass Google's verification process:

1. **Business verification**: business name, address, and phone number confirmed
2. **Insurance verification**: proof of active commercial general liability insurance (minimum coverage varies by province and category)
3. **Licence verification**: relevant trade licence (plumbing, electrical, HVAC technician certification) in your province
4. **Background checks**: identity verification for business owners and key employees

In Canada, the process typically takes 2–6 weeks. Once approved, your business displays the **Google Guaranteed** badge — a shield icon that signals to consumers that Google has verified your credentials. For homeowners choosing between two unfamiliar contractors, this badge is a meaningful trust signal.

**LSA budget model**: You set a weekly budget based on how many leads you want. Google manages bids automatically within that budget, optimising for the best leads for your business type and service area.

### When to Use LSA vs. Google Ads

**LSA is typically preferred** for:
- Core emergency services (burst pipes, furnace failure, power outage) where call intent is immediate
- Business categories that Google has specifically verified (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, locksmith, moving, cleaning)
- Small businesses with limited time to manage complex Google Ads campaigns
- Markets where LSA ads appear prominently and competitors are using them

**Traditional Google Ads (Search) is preferred** for:
- Service categories not yet covered by LSA in Canada
- Detailed keyword targeting control (bidding higher on specific high-value queries)
- Landing page A/B testing and granular conversion tracking
- Display remarketing to website visitors
- YouTube pre-roll ads for brand awareness campaigns

The optimal strategy for most home services businesses with budgets over $1,500/month: **run both**. LSA captures the highest-intent emergency queries; Google Ads handles scheduled service requests, competitor conquest, and remarketing.

## Keyword Strategy: Emergency vs. Scheduled Service Keywords

### Emergency Keywords: High Intent, High Value, High Cost-Per-Click

Emergency keywords represent immediate, urgent demand. The searcher is making a decision in the next few minutes:

- "emergency plumber Ottawa"
- "furnace repair same day"
- "24 hour electrician Toronto"
- "water heater broken repair now"
- "drain backup flooding"

CPCs for these terms range from $15 to $50+ in major Canadian markets. But the conversion rate is also high — someone searching "emergency plumber" and calling is likely to book, and emergency calls typically carry premium rates ($200–$500+ minimum call-out fee).

**Bidding approach**: maximise impression share for emergency keywords during business hours. Consider automated bidding strategies (Maximise Conversions or Target CPA) once you have 30+ conversions tracked in a 30-day period.

### Scheduled Service Keywords: Lower Intent, Lower CPC, Longer Conversion Cycle

These queries indicate a future need or price research:

- "furnace tune-up cost"
- "central air conditioning installation"
- "electrical panel upgrade quote"
- "annual plumbing inspection"

Lower CPCs ($5–$20), but the searcher is comparing options. They need to see your reviews, your website, your pricing, and your differentiation before calling. A landing page with clear service descriptions, transparent pricing (or "get a free quote"), and prominent reviews is essential for this keyword category.

**Recommended budget allocation**: 65% to emergency keywords, 35% to scheduled service keywords. Adjust based on your business's capacity — if you're already fully booked on emergencies and want to build the scheduled service side, shift the ratio.

## Geographic Targeting: Service Radius and City Names

### Radius Targeting

Set your ad serving radius to match your actual service territory. For most residential service businesses:
- **Urban markets (Ottawa, Calgary, Halifax)**: 20–35 km radius
- **Rural or spread-out service areas**: up to 75 km, but monitor travel time impact on profitability
- **Dense city cores (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal)**: 15–20 km maximum, as traffic makes large radius uneconomical

**Negative geographic targeting**: explicitly exclude areas outside your service radius where you know Google's geo-targeting isn't perfectly precise. Postal code exclusion is more accurate than radius alone in areas with unusual geography.

### Location-Specific Keywords

Include geographic modifiers in keyword lists:
- "plumber [neighbourhood name]"
- "HVAC contractor [city]"
- "electrician [suburb] [province]"

Searchers who add their neighbourhood to a search often have stronger local intent and are further along in the decision process. These terms typically have lower competition and better conversion rates than generic city-level terms.

### Bid Adjustments by Location

If certain postal codes or neighbourhoods in your service area convert better (higher-value homes requiring larger jobs, demographic alignment with premium services), apply **location bid adjustments** of +15% to +40% for those zones. This increases your ad position specifically where it matters most.

## Call Tracking and Attribution

For home services, phone calls are the primary conversion action — typically representing 70–85% of all leads for emergency and scheduled services combined. Without call tracking, you cannot attribute leads to specific campaigns, keywords, or ads.

### Call Tracking Setup

Services like **CallRail**, **WhatConverts**, **CallTrackingMetrics**, or **Ruler Analytics** assign unique phone numbers to each traffic source:
- One number for Google Ads (emergency campaign)
- One number for Google Ads (scheduled services campaign)
- One number for organic/direct (your regular website number)
- One number for LSA

Every call to any of these numbers is recorded, attributed to its source, and reported. After 30 days, you know: Google Ads emergency campaign generated 18 calls this month; 13 were booked jobs averaging $380; cost per acquired customer was $104.

### Importing Calls as Google Ads Conversions

Connect your call tracking service to Google Ads to import call events as conversions. Set the minimum call duration threshold (typically 45–90 seconds — calls shorter than this are usually wrong numbers or informational queries) so you're tracking qualified call conversions, not all calls.

This data feeds Google's smart bidding algorithms — Target CPA bidding becomes dramatically more effective when powered by real conversion data from tracked calls.

### Form Conversion Tracking

For scheduled service quote requests submitted via web form, configure **Google Ads conversion tracking** via your website's thank-you page or Google Tag Manager. Even if forms represent only 15–20% of leads, tracking them provides additional signal for smart bidding.

## Seasonal Bidding: HVAC Summer/Winter Peaks

HVAC businesses are the quintessential example of sharp seasonal demand patterns, with two distinct peaks:

**Winter heating season** (November–March in most of Canada):
- Furnace repair and replacement demand peaks
- Emergency calls surge during extreme cold events
- "Furnace emergency repair" CPCs increase significantly during peak demand

**Summer cooling season** (June–August):
- Air conditioning installation and repair
- Emergency calls during heat waves

**Seasonal bidding strategy**:

4–6 weeks before each season's start:
- Increase budgets by 40–60% for seasonally relevant keywords
- Launch seasonal campaigns with season-specific messaging ("Beat the heat — AC tune-up special")
- Increase bid adjustments for emergency terms

During peak season:
- Monitor cost-per-acquisition daily (CPCs rise, but so should conversion rates)
- Ensure your call answering capacity matches ad spend — wasted leads are the most expensive

Post-season:
- Reduce seasonal campaign budgets by 50–70%
- Maintain year-round budgets on evergreen keywords (annual tune-up, inspection, replacement)
- Use the quieter season to build review volume and improve landing pages

## Competitor Conquest Campaigns

A competitive tactic: bid on **competitor brand name keywords**. When a homeowner searches "Peterson Plumbing Ottawa," your ad can appear above their result.

**What you can do**:
- Bid on competitor brand names as keywords
- Set higher bids for competitor-name queries if they represent high-value customers

**What you cannot do** (Google policy):
- Use the competitor's trademarked name in your ad headline or description
- Mislead searchers into thinking they're clicking on the competitor's ad

Your ad must clearly identify your own business. The ad copy should highlight your differentiation: "Ottawa's Fastest Response — No Call-Out Fee," "Licensed Plumber — Available Tonight."

Competitor conquest campaigns work best when you have a clear, demonstrable advantage over the targeted competitor (significantly better reviews, lower prices, faster response time).

## Budget Management for Small Home Services Businesses

Most independent plumbers, HVAC technicians, and electricians operate with monthly advertising budgets of $500 to $2,500. Here's how to maximise returns at each budget level:

**$500–$800/month**: Focus exclusively on LSA. Lower management complexity, pay-per-lead model, faster path to verified leads. Supplement with free Google Business Profile optimisation.

**$800–$1,500/month**: Add a tightly focused Google Search campaign targeting 10–15 emergency keywords in your primary service area. One campaign, one ad group per service type, daily monitoring.

**$1,500–$2,500/month**: Run both LSA and Google Ads. Add a scheduled services campaign and basic remarketing (showing ads to previous website visitors who didn't call). Start A/B testing landing pages.

**Universal principles at any budget**:
- Set **dayparting**: limit ad serving to hours when you can actually answer the phone (or adjust bids down by 80% during overnight hours if you don't offer 24/7 service)
- Monitor **search term reports weekly** and add irrelevant terms as negative keywords
- Never let Google spend more than your daily budget allows — use strict daily budget caps, not monthly budget management
