Meridian Law Group: AI-Powered Contract Review

Meridian, a mid-sized corporate law firm with 120 attorneys, was spending 30–40% of associate time on first-pass contract review — work that was billable but increasingly commoditized. They needed to either lower costs or redirect that time to higher-value advisory work.

We deployed a custom RAG-based contract analysis system trained on Meridian's 15-year clause library. The system flags risks, suggests standard alternatives, and generates first-draft redlines — all in the firm's own voice and precedent style.

NovaTech Retail: AI Personalization at Scale

NovaTech Retail operates an e-commerce platform with 2.4M active customers and a catalog of 85,000 SKUs. Despite strong traffic, conversion rates were flat and average order value was declining. Their existing recommendation engine was rules-based and hadn't been updated in three years.

We replaced their legacy recommendation system with a real-time ML personalization engine, integrated dynamic email triggers, and rebuilt their search ranking algorithm. The entire platform now adapts to individual user behavior in real time.

Vantage Health: AI Documentation & Clinical Efficiency

Vantage Health operates 12 outpatient clinics with 340 clinical staff. Administrative burden — particularly documentation — was consuming nearly 3 hours per clinician per day, driving burnout and limiting patient capacity. Previous EHR optimization had hit its ceiling.

We deployed ambient AI documentation that transcribes and structures clinical notes in real time, integrated with their existing EHR. Combined with an AI-powered patient intake and scheduling system, we fundamentally changed the administrative load on clinical staff.
